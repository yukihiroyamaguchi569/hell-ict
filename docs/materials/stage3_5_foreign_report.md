# Unexplained Febrile Cluster in Eastern Ling Province: Preliminary Report

Eastern Ling Infectious Disease Surveillance Office — Cluster Bulletin No. 18

## Overview

Between 3 and 14 May, three hospitals in Eastern Ling Province reported 19 patients with fever and negative initial testing for common pathogens. This preliminary report summarizes clinical records and follow-up interviews with patients and contacts. It does not establish a causative agent, route of transmission, or common source.

## Clinical Course

All 19 patients had a recorded temperature of at least 38.0°C. Symptoms other than fever varied at first presentation. Fatigue, headache, and reduced appetite were documented intermittently.

Follow-up interviews found that 15 of 19 patients had experienced deep ocular pain 8 to 18 hours before fever onset. Eleven patients described unusual sensitivity to light. These symptoms were often recorded initially as eye strain or migraine.

## Contact Observations

Secondary illness was observed more often among people who had contact with a patient on the day before that patient's fever began. Records of contact after fever onset, location, and duration were incomplete. These observations cannot determine whether pre-fever contact contributed to transmission.

## Limitations and Immediate Request

This is a small observational report. It remains unknown whether ocular symptoms indicate infection with the causative agent or whether person-to-person transmission is occurring. Healthcare facilities are asked to record symptoms and contact history that precede unexplained fever.
